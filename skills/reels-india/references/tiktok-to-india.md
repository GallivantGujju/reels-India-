# TikTok / US default → India adaptation (English content)

Most viral short-form skills assume TikTok and a US viewer. TikTok has been banned in India since 2020 [verify current status],
so Indian short-form runs on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, and sharing often moves to WhatsApp.

| TikTok / US default | India adaptation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Post to TikTok's For You page | Route to Reels, Shorts or FB Reels (`platform-router.md`) | TikTok isn't available in India |
| "Send this to a friend" via in-app share | Forward CTA aimed at a named WhatsApp group or person; score with `forward-test.md` | A lot of Indian sharing is private, on WhatsApp ("dark social") |
| Trending audio is the reach lever (48–72 h window per Vyral) | Use trending audio only if it's licensed for the account type; make sure the Reel still works with sound off | Brand accounts have limited audio libraries; many viewers watch muted |
| US references: $, 401k, Venmo, Black Friday, college applications | ₹, SIP/PF/FD, UPI, Diwali sale, board exams/JEE/placements (`english-for-india.md`) | Relatability drives watch time and forwards |
| Native-speaker idiom and slang ("no cap", "it's giving") | Plain, clear English; short sentences; on-screen text repeats key words | Mixed English proficiency; second-language readers skim |
| US holiday calendar | Indian calendar: festivals, wedding season, IPL, exam results, salary day (`india-moments.md`) | Indian demand and attention follow these moments |
| Filmed and checked on a flagship phone, fast Wi-Fi | Check on a budget Android on mobile data: large high-contrast text, small file (`production-checklist.md`) | That's how much of the audience watches [verify against your analytics] |
| Creator-first, edgy humour | Family-group-safe framing for anything meant to be forwarded | A forward has to be safe to send to Mom or the office group |
| Finance "hacks" and income claims | Check finance or investment claims against current regulations before publishing | Indian ad rules for finance (SEBI, ASCI, RBI) are strict; not encoded here |
| One platform, one cut | Reels cut + Shorts cut (searchable title, link to long video) + FB Reels cut if the router says so | Different platforms reach different Indian audiences |

Keep from the defaults: the 3-second hook, the hook batch, one idea per video, a frame change every beat, loops, one CTA,
captions as search, no watermarks, honest framing (`best-practices.md`).
