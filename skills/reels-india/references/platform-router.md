# Reels vs Shorts vs Facebook Reels (router)

All of this is heuristic and must be [verify]-ed against the account's own analytics. No platform-share figures are claimed here.

| Signal in the brief | Lean towards | Why (heuristic) |
|---|---|---|
| Urban 18–30, lifestyle, fashion, food, D2C | Instagram Reels | Discovery plus DM/WhatsApp sends; strong for brands |
| Explainers, finance, tech, exam prep, "how to" | YouTube Shorts, paired with long-form | Search intent and a long-form funnel; Shorts feed the channel |
| 35+, family audience, smaller towns, devotional, local news | Facebook Reels (cross-post) | Often reaches older and family audiences [verify for your page] |
| B2B, founders, careers | Reels + Shorts, with a LinkedIn video cut | Professional audience splits across platforms |
| Brand new account, no data | Post to Reels and Shorts for 4 weeks, then compare | Let the data decide |

## Adapting per platform

- **Reels**: forward CTA, keyword caption, Trial Reels for cold tests (upstream)
- **Shorts**: a searchable title, the first frame matching the title, an end screen to the related long video, a pinned comment
- **Facebook Reels**: slower pace, larger text, a plainer hook, family-safe framing

## Decide and record

Output the chosen platforms, the reason, and what metric to compare after 2–4 weeks (follows per 1,000 views, shares per reach, subscribers gained).
