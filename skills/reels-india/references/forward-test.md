# WhatsApp forward test

Question: **would someone forward this to a specific WhatsApp group or person?** Indian sharing often happens off-platform,
so this is a proxy for sends per reach. Score before you publish. The 7/10 publish bar is my working rule, not a measured threshold.

## Score each 0–2 (max 10)

| # | Check | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Named recipient**: can you name who gets it ("my flatmate", "the office group", "Mom")? | no one | vague | specific person or group |
| 2 | **Social currency**: does forwarding make the sender look smart, funny or caring? | no | a bit | clearly |
| 3 | **Useful payoff**: a tip, number, checklist or warning they'd want to keep | none | generic | concrete and saveable |
| 4 | **Safe to forward**: nothing embarrassing, political or risky for the family/office group | risky | borderline | safe |
| 5 | **Understandable out of context**: makes sense with sound off and no caption, at a glance in a chat | no | partly | yes |

## Fixes by low score

- 1 low → rewrite the CTA as "send this to the friend who ___" with one named situation
- 2 low → add the "I knew this before you" angle or a laugh line
- 3 low → turn the advice into a number or a 3-step list on screen
- 4 low → cut anything that would get the sender a reply from an uncle
- 5 low → put the whole idea in the first-frame text

Output: score, the weakest check, and one rewritten line per weak check.
