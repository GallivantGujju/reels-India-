# Sound-off and low-end phone production checklist

Many Indian viewers watch with sound off, on budget Android phones, on patchy data. Treat these as working rules of thumb
and check them against your own retention data [verify].

## On-screen text
- [ ] First frame carries the hook in 3–7 words, readable in under 2 seconds
- [ ] Text large enough to read on a small screen (aim for the text block to fill a good share of frame width; test on the cheapest phone you can find)
- [ ] High contrast (white with a dark outline/box, or black on yellow); no thin fonts
- [ ] Inside the safe zone. Meta's Ads Guide for Facebook Reels ads says to leave roughly 14% of the top, 35% of the bottom, and 6% of each side free of text, logos, and other key elements (https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/facebook-facebook-reels, which points at the safe-zone note https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490). That is an **ad spec**, not a measured margin for organic Reels. Use it as the working margin, and say so.
- [ ] Every key spoken line appears on screen; captions are burned in or auto-captions are checked for errors

## Visual
- [ ] Bright, even lighting; face and product visible in the first frame
- [ ] One subject per frame; no busy backgrounds
- [ ] No third-party watermarks (upstream originality rule)

## File and playback
- [ ] Exported at platform-recommended resolution; reasonable file size for slow connections
- [ ] Loops cleanly; no dead air at the start
- [ ] Watched once on mobile data on a low-end phone before posting

## Audio (for sound-on viewers)
- [ ] Voice clear over music; music ducked under speech
- [ ] Licensed audio for the account type (upstream)

## Editing pairing
For animated captions and explainer edits, see srvjha/reel-animated-explainer (a Claude Code plugin using Remotion). Check its licence and themes first.
