# Shared best practices from the most-installed short-form skills

Where the top short-form video skills agree (sources in `sources.md`). Written in my own words; no upstream text copied except the
unchanged Vyral Reels file shipped in this folder. Thresholds credited to a source are that source's working numbers, not mine, and are [verify].

## Hooks
- The first 2–3 seconds decide the video. No greeting, logo, "in this video" or intro animation. (All sources.)
- Write a batch of hooks (3–10) across different archetypes, pick one, film 2–3 variants. (Vyral viral-hooks; Jake's ig-reel scores 3.)
- A hook has three layers: visual, spoken and on-screen text. Write the on-screen line separately from the spoken line. (Vyral viral-hooks; Jake's ig-reel.)
- Specific beats clever: concrete nouns, numbers, named things. (Vyral viral-hooks; Jake's ig-reel "numbers over adjectives".)
- The payoff must match the promise, or retention and trust drop. (Vyral viral-hooks.)

## Retention and pacing
- Completion and watch time are the gate; likes matter less. (Vyral TikTok, Reels, Shorts.)
- One idea per video. If there are two, make two videos. (Jake's ig-reel.)
- Change the frame every beat; no static shot longer than a few seconds. (Jake's ig-reel flags beats over 4 s.)
- 15–45 s is the working range for most Reels; 30–45 s for most Shorts. (Jake's ig-reel; Vyral Shorts.)
- Design a loop: the last line sets up a replay of the first. (Vyral Shorts; Jake's ig-reel.)
- Diagnose from the retention curve, not views. (Vyral Shorts; Jake's yt-retention.)

## CTA and distribution
- Shares and saves outweigh likes; build for "send this to a friend" or "I'll need this later". (Vyral TikTok and Reels.)
- One ask at the end. Never "follow for more". (Jake's ig-reel; Vyral TikTok anti-patterns.)
- Captions are search: front-load the first line; 3–5 specific hashtags, not 20. (Vyral TikTok and Reels.)

## Packaging
- No other platform's watermark on cross-posts; give every cross-post a new hook. (Vyral TikTok, Reels.)
- On-screen text: six words or fewer per card, up from frame 1, inside the safe zone, never where the caption sits; burn in captions for the body. (Jake's ig-reel; exact pixel margins in that skill [verify].)
- A Short cut from a long video needs a new first line and must stand alone without the video around it. (Jake's yt-shorts.)
- Shorts: link to a related long video where one exists; the Short → long-form click is the valuable signal. (Vyral Shorts.)

## Honesty
- Pattern-matching, not prediction. Never promise virality, never invent numbers. Leave `{{your number}}` when one is missing. (All sources.)
